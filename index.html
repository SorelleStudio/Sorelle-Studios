Questions

Things people usually ask.

How long does a website project usually take? +

Most projects take two to four weeks from kickoff to launch — depending on scope, the number of pages, and how quickly you can review work between cycles. Every proposal includes a fixed timeline upfront.

How does the lead intelligence actually work? +

You give us a brief — sector, geography, company size, decision-maker role — and we build a custom pipeline of qualified, enriched contacts tailored to that profile. You get a clean spreadsheet (or CRM import) of businesses you can actually approach.

How fresh is the data in the pipelines? +

Every pipeline is built fresh on order — not pulled from a stale database. Emails are verified at delivery time, and we flag any contact we couldn't confirm.

Do you work with clients outside the UK and Spain? +

Yes. We're based between London and Madrid but work with businesses across the UK, EU, and occasionally further afield. Time zones, languages, and local market specifics aren't a problem.

Can I get both services in one engagement? +

Yes — and it's the most popular setup. We build the site and the pipeline together, so by the time you launch, you already have a list of qualified prospects to point at it. Bundled engagements come with a meaningful discount.

What happens after the project launches? +

Every project includes twelve months of ongoing care — small tweaks, content updates, performance monitoring, and quick-turn fixes if anything breaks. After that you can renew or move on. No lock-in.

Privacy

A short note on your data.

Sorelle Studio respects your privacy. We collect only the information you share with us directly — through email, enquiry forms, or correspondence — and use it solely to respond to your message and deliver the work you've engaged us for.

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties. Anything you send us stays between us, and we apply industry-standard practices to protect it.

If you'd like your details removed from our records at any time, write to sales@sorellestudio.org and we'll handle it promptly.

This site uses minimal analytics to understand how visitors engage with our work — no personal data is tracked, and no cookies are set without your knowledge.
