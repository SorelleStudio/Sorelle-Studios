Privacy

A short note on your data.

Sorelle Studio respects your privacy. We collect only the information you share with us directly — through email, enquiry forms, or correspondence — and use it solely to respond to your message and deliver the work you've engaged us for.

We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties. Anything you send us stays between us, and we apply industry-standard practices to protect it.

If you'd like your details removed from our records at any time, write to sales@sorellestudio.org and we'll handle it promptly.

This site uses minimal analytics to understand how visitors engage with our work — no personal data is tracked, and no cookies are set without your knowledge.
